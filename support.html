Game Day North

Game Day North shows when your teams play and which Canadian service carries each game, with scores you can hide.

Report a problem or a wrong channel. Open an issue on the Game Day North issue tracker. Say which game, the date, and what you saw. A link to the broadcaster's own listing helps most.

Why does a game say Carrier TBC?

No Canadian rights holder could be confirmed for that competition. Rather than guess, the app says so. It changes as soon as a broadcaster or league announces the deal.

What do Confirmed and Expected mean?

Confirmed comes from a league or broadcaster announcement or a published listing for that game. Expected follows a known pattern, such as a national window or regional rights, but has not been listed for that game yet. Inside five days, only confirmed carriers are shown. The rights table at the bottom of the app links each rule to its source.

Why is a channel wrong for my area?

Regional hockey games depend on where you live, and some games are blacked out in or outside a team's region. The app shows the regional carrier for the team; check the broadcaster's app for blackout rules where you are.

Scores look old or a game is missing

The note at the bottom of the app says where the schedule came from and how old it is. Fixtures refresh every few hours and anything in progress is read live. Offline, the app shows its last saved schedule and says so.

How do I avoid spoilers?

Turn scores off. Results and the In the Race section stay hidden until you turn scores back on.

Where does the data come from?

Fixtures and scores come from public sports feeds: ESPN, World Rugby and, for cycling, Wikipedia. Canadian broadcast rights are researched by hand from league and broadcaster announcements and checked weekly. The privacy policy lists every service the app contacts.

Does the app stream games?

No. It tells you where a game is on and links to that service. You need your own subscription to watch.

Game Day North is an independent app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any league, team or broadcaster. Team, league and broadcaster names are used only to identify them.
