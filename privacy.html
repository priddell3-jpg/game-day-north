Privacy policy

Game Day North · effective 24 November 2026

Game Day North collects no personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no crash reporting. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us.

What stays on your device

To remember your choices between visits, the app keeps these on your phone (or in your browser, on the website), and nowhere else:

  • the teams, players, nations and competitions you follow, and the ones you hide;
  • the streaming and TV services you say you have;
  • whether scores are shown or hidden, and which results you have already seen;
  • games you have saved, and how you have arranged the In the Race section;
  • your alert setting, and in the iPhone app the game reminders it schedules, which your phone delivers itself;
  • the most recent schedule file, so the app still works offline.

We cannot see any of it. Deleting the app, or clearing this site's data in your browser, removes it.

Which services your device contacts

To show fixtures, scores and where each game is on, your device asks these services for public sports data. It sends no information about you, but like any internet request, each one receives your device's IP address and basic technical details, handled under that service's own privacy policy.

ServiceAddressWhat it provides
Our website, hosted by Vercelgame-day-north.vercel.appThe schedule file, Canadian broadcast rights and tennis scores
ESPNsite.api.espn.comLive scores and game status
World Rugbyapi.wr-rims-prod.pulselive.comRugby fixtures and scores

We do not use these requests to identify you, and we run no analytics on them.

Links and sharing

Tapping a source or a broadcaster opens that website in your browser, where its own privacy policy applies. If you use the share link, your team choices travel in the link itself, so they reach only the people you send it to.

Children

Game Day North is a general audience app. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. A change that collected any personal data would be announced in the app before it took effect.

Contact

Questions about privacy are welcome on the support page.

Game Day North is an independent app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any league, team or broadcaster.
